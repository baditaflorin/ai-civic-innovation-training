Kumasi Road Check

Worked example · Kumasi Metropolitan, Ghana

One road in five is in poor condition. Which ones are ours?

The official figure is a national total. It does not say which streets. This page starts the list for one district, from open data anyone can download.

21%

of Ghana's road network was rated poor at the end of 2025.

47% good · 32% fair · 21% poor, on about 94,000 km. World Bank figure as reported in January 2026; the Roads Minister gave 44, 34 and 22.

The district map: 315 km of streets

Every drivable road in Kumasi Metropolitan, coloured by what volunteers recorded about its surface in OpenStreetMap. Numbered pins mark the 20 unpaved stretches to photograph first.

Paved, 219 km (70%) Unpaved, 52 km (16%) Nobody recorded it, 44 km (14%)

Unpaved does not mean bad, and paved does not mean good: a tarred road can be full of potholes. The map says where to look. Only a dated photo says what is there.

Draft message to the assembly

To the Urban Roads unit, Kumasi Metropolitan Assembly,

We are a youth team that mapped road surfaces in the metropolis using OpenStreetMap. Of 315 km of streets, 52 km are recorded as unpaved and 44 km have no record at all. We photographed 20 of the unpaved stretches on [dates] and attach each photo with its location.

Could you tell us which of these 20 are on this year's maintenance plan, and when work is scheduled?

Draft only. Nothing has been sent. Add the photos and dates, and confirm the right office, before sending.
